Common questions

Where are my saves?

On the device you played them on. Presidenter stores your progress locally. Your career-best day count can also sync through Game Center if you are signed in.

A reign reset itself, or a card I expected did not appear.

The deck deliberately varies across reigns to keep each run fresh, so you will not see every card every time. If you think something broke, email us with as much detail as you can and we will look into it.

Can I export a record of my reign?

Yes. From the game-over screen, open the chronicle and tap the export icon. It saves a file to a destination you choose (Files, Mail, Messages, and so on).

How do the optional AI and voice features work?

Both are off by default. If you want AI-generated cards or high-quality read-aloud voices, you supply your own API key in Settings. Your key stays in the iOS Keychain on your device. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Does the game cost anything or show ads?

Presidenter is free, with no ads, no in-app purchases, and no tracking.
